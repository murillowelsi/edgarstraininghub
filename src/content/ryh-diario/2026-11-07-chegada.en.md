The mission gets under way on Saturday, even before landing. During the flight, a Bible reading opens the way to a deep conversation about faith, purpose and overcoming with the passenger in the next seat.

Once in Porto, with essential logistical support from local friends and from *Barbosa Bike Shop*, the bike is collected and the first social action gets going straight away. At a local supermarket, two full grocery shops are made for a total of **€259**, filled with essential food, hygiene products and even a few chocolates. The groceries are handed to trusted contacts in Vila Nova de Gaia to be passed on to two neighbours in a vulnerable situation — one of them in hospital that day. The trip has barely begun and the purpose is already taking shape.
