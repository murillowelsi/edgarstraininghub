The journey begins even before the first pedal stroke on Portuguese soil. At the *Renovatro Bikes* workshop, the bike gets a full service before being carefully taken apart and packed into its travel case (*bike case*).

While the final training session takes place in temperatures close to 3 °C — adding up to 47 km in 1h45 of intense effort —, the best news of the preparation arrives: the fundraising campaign reaches the **1,000 pounds** mark, securing the budget needed to support **seven families** along the route. Between bags, equipment and supplements, emotion takes over when collecting the bike from the workshop on Friday night. Everything is ready, but the greatest expectation is not in the kilometres to ride, but in the people God will place along the way.
