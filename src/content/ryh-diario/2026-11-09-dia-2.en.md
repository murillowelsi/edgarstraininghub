The second day shows that the true meaning of the project happens off the saddle. First thing in the morning, at a *Pingo Doce* supermarket in Chaves, meeting **three families** in difficulty transforms the routine of the trip. With the donation money, three full grocery shops are made — worth **€66**, **€70** and **€98** — including specific food for a mother with two young children (aged 8 and 12) and support for a Colombian citizen going through a difficult time.

With a full heart, the official start follows at the **Kilometre 0 of the National Road 2** marker (which links Chaves to Faro). However, after **45 km** cycled, the weather closes in drastically: heavy rain, gusts of wind and thick fog reduce visibility to almost zero. Honouring the commitment to always put safety first in the face of heavy traffic, the bike is loaded into the support car. The team then heads to Vila Real and Castro Daire to stamp the N2 passport and prepare for the next day.
