The final stage took us to Faro, at the southern tip of the country, where we supported one more family.

And so the north-to-south crossing comes to an end: a 879.4 km route, more than 11,000 m of climbing and 13 families helped in eight towns — Porto, Chaves, Castro Daire, Vila Nova de Poiares, Vila de Rei, Mora, Ferreira do Alentejo and Faro.

Legs tired, heart full. Thank you to everyone who was part of this journey.
