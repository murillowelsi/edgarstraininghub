We set off early after breakfast and a coffee, trying to stay ahead of the rain. A strong wind weighed on our legs from the very first hours. Along the way we stopped for another coffee and to get our Estrada Nacional 2 passport stamped.

The further south we went, the drier and more open the landscape became. It was around 130 km to our accommodation, with a short gravel stretch at the end.

In Ferreira do Alentejo we went food shopping for a lovely family and a lady. What began as a simple act of kindness became a deeply moving experience as we heard more about their lives. We wanted to help quietly and respectfully, without making anyone feel exposed.
