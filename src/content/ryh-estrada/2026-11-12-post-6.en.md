We began the day thanking Rosa and her wonderful staff for the lovely time we had staying in Vila de Rei during the storm.

We then arrived in Mora, where we met Bia and her husband. They planted the church in Mora 25 years ago, and today they will be giving groceries to a member of their church — a beautifully timed blessing. It was so lovely to sit down, get to know them and share a little time together.

Today we cycled 103 km through a mix of rain and sunshine. We have just two more destinations ahead on our journey through Portugal.
