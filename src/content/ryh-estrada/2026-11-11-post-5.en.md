What a truly special day! We had the privilege of blessing two beautiful families in Castro Daire, and we also stopped in Vila Nova de Poiares, where we met Pastor Rodrigo. He will carry that blessing to another family who used to live in England a couple of years ago.

We finished the day in Vila de Rei, where we met a lovely man who is blind and raised three children. What a beautiful reminder of God’s love and faithfulness.

Thank you, Lord, for every moment You allowed us to be used to bless people. And a heartfelt thank you to everyone who donated and has been part of this journey: your generosity is touching lives.
