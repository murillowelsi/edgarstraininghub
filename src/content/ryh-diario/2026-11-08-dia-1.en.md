The first official day on the road is on Sunday and demands everything from body and mind. After a nutritional boost first thing in the morning and a prayer asking for protection from accidents, the departure takes place in good company. However, the geography of northern Portugal commands respect from early on: a persistent headwind and brutal ramps reaching **11% gradient**, with continuous climbs of more than half an hour.

Along the way, the first tests of resilience appear: an unexpected detour on the route forces a turn back and the phone battery threatens to run out, requiring a quick stop to recharge and to navigate by GPS. After more than five hours of intense effort in the cold, about 15 km from the destination, the body reaches its physical limit (*"hitting the wall"*), with shaking and extreme exhaustion. With determination, the last kilometres are completed to the accommodation, closing the hardest day ever faced on a bike.
