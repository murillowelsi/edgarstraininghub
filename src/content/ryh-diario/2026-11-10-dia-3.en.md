With the country under a red weather warning and strong storms preventing safe progress on two wheels, the third day is entirely dedicated to transparency and social action in the inland towns.

* **Full Transparency:** Right at the start of the day, all the invoices for the purchases made so far are presented publicly, reinforcing the commitment that 100% of donations are turned into food (while accommodation, fuel and personal expenses are covered independently).
* **Institutional Support in Castro Daire:** While looking for families in need in the town, the team connects with a local charity. Respecting the beneficiaries' privacy, **two large baskets of groceries** are bought and delivered directly to the charity's headquarters for immediate distribution.
* **Meeting at the Fire Station:** At the next stop, contact with Pastor Rodrigo — who works at the local fire station — opens doors to help a couple (he is Portuguese, she is Brazilian) who returned to Portugal after 30 years in the United Kingdom, but who face serious health problems that prevent them from working. The groceries are delivered at the fire station, accompanied by a moment of prayer.
* **The Last Meeting of the Day:** Already at nightfall, when collecting the keys to the local accommodation, a simple question to the host reveals the story of a **blind father** abandoned four years ago, who is raising **three children** on his own. Without hesitation, the team goes back to the supermarket, fills a shopping trolley and delivers everything in person to that family's door — closing the day with the certainty that every stop had a greater purpose.
